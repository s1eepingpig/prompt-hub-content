# Cold Outreach Email

Write a first-contact email to a specific recipient. Ask the user for: who they are, who the recipient is, and the single outcome they want. If any is missing, ask before writing.

## Rules

- **Under 120 words.** No preamble.
- **Subject line:** specific, lowercase, no hype, no clickbait. Never "Quick question?".
- **Open with the recipient,** not with us. Reference something concrete about their situation that the user supplied.
- **One value sentence.** What changes for them, stated plainly. No adjectives like "revolutionary" or "seamless".
- **One ask.** A small, specific next step (a 15-minute call, a yes/no reply). Never ask for "a quick chat sometime".
- **No false familiarity.** Do not claim we know them, used their product, or were referred unless the user said so.
- **No flattery openers** ("I love what you're building"). Get to the point.

## Output

```text
Subject: ...

<body>

<signature — user's name only>
```
